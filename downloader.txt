Track tab:
- Paste one Spotify or SoundCloud track link.
- app extracts title, artist, and artwork.
- app searches YouTube automatically with YoutubeKit ios .
- If a match is found, the app builts a music file with artwork/title/artist embeded on the mp3 file and downloaded into the phone’s app local storage.
- If no match is found, the app switches to Custom tab automatically with the extracted title, artist, and artwork filled in.
- Real backend errors are shown exactly.
Custom tab:
- Artist
- Track name
- Audio link
The audio link accepts either a direct audio file or a YouTube/SoundCloud link processed by youtubekit.
